## The common part (all the engines with a settings screen)

- **engine-specs `^0.4.1` → `^0.6.0`**. The app imports the helpers from `../engine-specs.js`, the
  runtime copy the Makefile already ships beside the SDK.
- **English and Spanish**:
  - every string of the app (today Spanish, inline) moves into one `{ en, es }` table;
  - the table is read through `host.locale ?? resolveLocale()`;
  - SDK errors stay English and are shown as a detail under the app's own words.
- **Pause menu**: Continuar, the engine's entries, **Ajustes**, **Pantalla completa**, Volver al
  lanzador (UX.md rule 4).
- **Ajustes**: `createSettingsScreen()`.
  - Rows come from `settingsModel()` over the SDK's `config()` / `options()` descriptors (only those
    with a `label`), plus `SHARED_SETTINGS`.
  - A live change is applied at once.
  - A non-live one is saved and passed at the next start; its row says "Se aplica al reiniciar".
  - Choices are saved per engine with `saveSettings('snes9x')` and read at mount with
    `loadSettings()`.
- **Fullscreen**: `createFullscreenToggle(root)` (Cmd+Enter / Alt+Enter, Select + Y), plus the
  pause-menu entry.
- **Performance**:
  - `createWakeLock()`, held while playing when "Mantener la pantalla encendida" is on (default
    on);
  - `createPerfCounter()` when "Mostrar rendimiento" is on: fed once per emulated frame where the
    SDK reports frames, else counting display frames.
- **Controls and audio pacing**: per engine, below.
- **Checks**:
  - `make test` / build;
  - in Chromium, with the owner's test ROMs where needed: the pause menu, every setting applied (or
    noted as on restart), saved across a reload, fullscreen by key and by menu, the counter, en / es;
  - a phone width.
- **Release**: minor.

## snes9x

- **Settings**: the 11 catalog rows in `src/snes9x.options.ts` (image filtering, aspect 4:3 / 1:1,
  crop overscan, volume, DSP interpolation, region, sprites per line, Super FX clock, multitap…).
  - They become `label` / `group` / `valueLabels` with Spanish added.
  - `requiresReset` rows stay non-live.
  - The old in-engine ESC menu (`snes9x.menu.ts`) stays off.
- **Controls**: feasible.
  - `src/snes9x.core.ts` maps controls to `KeyboardEvent.code` (`'p1.up': 'ArrowUp'`…).
  - The app lists B, A, Y, X, L, R, Start, Select and the d-pad.
  - Gamepad buttons: to check, as for mGBA.
- **Audio pacing**: the core has its own `requestAnimationFrame` loop. Same check as mGBA.
- **Order**: second, after mGBA (same app family).

## Walkthrough (2026-11-17 20:30 UTC) — snes9x done (waits for engine-specs 0.6.1)

Adds: what was built, what changed from the plan, and the checks. It is mGBA's template
(mGBA-wasm, same record, walkthrough 19:40), adapted.

**Changed from the plan**:
- **No "Sincronización" row**: like mGBA, the core already paces by the audio clock, with a
  wall-clock fallback.
- **engine-specs 0.6.1 is needed** for bindings by physical key (wasm-gaming/engine-specs#7). The
  pin is `^0.6.0` until it is published; the release waits for it.

**SDK** (`src/`):
- `snes9x.core.ts`:
  - `setControls({ keys, buttons })`, live:
    - keys rebuild player 1's `KeyboardEvent.code` bindings. A key player 1 takes is removed from
      player 2's defaults (K was player 2's Down);
    - buttons replace the gamepad table, which was hard-coded until now. It applies to every pad
      (multitap included); the left stick still moves.
  - An `onFrames` hook after each batch of emulated frames.
  - `DEFAULT_KEYMAP` / `DEFAULT_PADMAP` exported.
- `snes9x.sdk.ts`: `play.setControls()`, `play.onFrames()`, and the types / defaults re-exported.
- `snes9x.options.ts`:
  - descriptors get `label` / `group` / `valueLabels`: English from the catalog, Spanish from a
    table beside it;
  - controllers go under Emulación; debug stays internal;
  - volume and the Super FX clock list their menu values as `enum`.

**App** (`src/app/app.js`, `app.css`): derived from mGBA's finished app. It has the same `STRINGS`
table, pause menu (Continuar, slots, Reiniciar, **Ajustes**, **Pantalla completa**, Volver al
lanzador), settings screen, fullscreen toggle, Wake Lock and counter. Differences from mGBA:
- the SNES buttons, A, B, X, Y, L, R, Start, Select and the d-pad;
- `escMenu: false` kept (snes9x's own overlay stays off);
- no frame-aspect line.

**Checks**:
- `tsc` clean; `make test` passes (15 tests); `make build-sdk` passes with engine-specs 0.6.1 copied
  locally. The WASM was taken from the published `@wasm-gaming/snes9x-wasm`.
- In Chromium, on a test host page with a homebrew 128 KiB LoROM (a counting loop):
  - the pause menu, en and es;
  - Ajustes with Vídeo / Audio / Emulación / Controles (Región "Se aplica al reiniciar");
  - → on Suavizado changes the canvas;
  - the counter in game;
  - A takes K, then B takes K and A gets X (swap); after the remap the core takes K and no longer
    S;
  - Alt+Enter enters fullscreen;
  - after a reload the settings and controls are back;
  - 390px in English.
